# How platforms let people launch a clothing brand

All sources checked on **2026-11-19**. **[unconfirmed]** means only a third-party or snippet source said it, or sources disagreed. Some help centres (Redbubble, parts of Printify) returned 403 to direct fetch. Those claims come from search-result excerpts of the official page and are marked *(snippet)*.

## Platform notes

**Fourthwall.** The closest structural match to "a brand." One login can run several storefronts, "each with separate settings, payouts, and product catalogs" ([help](https://help.fourthwall.com/hc/en-us/articles/41046894493339-Manage-Multiple-Storefronts-on-Fourthwall)). Each store gets one primary custom domain, and extra domains can only redirect to it. Each store needs its own Stripe Connect account. Agencies run many creator stores from a Partner Dashboard (same source). The Free plan is $0 and Pro is $15/mo. Catalog products carry a flat per-product cost with no percentage fee. Digital products pay 5% on Free and 0% on Pro. Memberships pay 5%. US cards cost 2.9% + $0.30. Free allows 2 team members and Pro is unlimited ([pricing](https://fourthwall.com/pricing)). Payouts are monthly, "in the first few days of each month," with a $25 minimum. They go through Stripe Connect, with PayPal or bill.com where Stripe isn't available ([help](https://help.fourthwall.com/frequently-asked-questions/payments-and-pricing/how-you-get-paid)). Collections can have their own page layouts ([help](https://help.fourthwall.com/getting-started/setting-up-your-shop/go-further/manage-multiple-storefronts-on-fourthwall)). AOP or cut-and-sew support: **[unconfirmed]**, since the pricing page doesn't mention it.

**Spring (ex-Teespring).** "Our platform only supports one store per Spring account." Custom domains are bought in-app as an annual subscription with no refunds ([FAQ](https://kb.spri.ng/creators/articles/custom-domain-faq-s.html)). Earnings are retail price minus base price. Payouts are monthly: a request made before the 21st is paid that month. The minimum is $25 and only shipped orders count. Methods are PayPal, Payoneer, and bank transfer for select users ([payouts](https://kb.spri.ng/creators/articles/payouts.html)). Spring's blog lists AOP sweatshirts and joggers ([blog](https://spring-blog.in.amaze.co/tag/design-store)) **[unconfirmed as current]**. A Pro tier at $19/mo is **[unconfirmed]**.

**Bonfire.** Built around campaigns and drops. Setup is free and you earn the selling price minus the base cost. The only listed fee is 8% on extra contributions ([fees](https://help.bonfire.com/en/articles/1614156-pricing-fees)). Payouts go via PayPal 2–8 business days after a campaign batch ends, and Bonfire covers payout fees ([payouts](https://help.bonfire.com/en/articles/1614157-how-payouts-work)). Custom domains and more storefront control sit in a separate paid "Bonfire Pro," with a Super tier at $24.92/mo ([pricing](https://www.bonfire.com/pro/pricing)) **[undated]**.

**Printful + Shopify.** You own everything and run everything. Shopify Basic is $39/mo ($29/mo billed yearly). Shopify Payments charges 2.9% + 30¢ online, and a third-party gateway adds a 2% fee ([Shopify pricing](https://www.shopify.com/pricing)). Paid orders auto-import to Printful, which charges your card or wallet straight away ([Printful help](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/6148086204316-How-do-I-manage-order-processing-and-fulfillment-in-Shopify)). Printful's own plans: Free gets unlimited stores and 10 Quick Stores. Growth is $24.99/mo, waived after $12K in annual sales. AOP inside labels cost $0.99, outside labels $2.49, and premium images $1 each ([Printful pricing](https://www.printful.com/pricing)). Printful offers cut-and-sew AOP track pants ([618](https://www.printful.com/custom/product/618)), a recycled track jacket ([801](https://www.printful.com/custom/product/801)) and joggers ([400](https://www.printful.com/custom/product/400)), so a full tracksuit exists.

**Printful Quick Stores.** Free and US-only. The customer pays Printful, and Printful pays out monthly via Stripe with a $25 minimum. A W-9 avoids 24% backup withholding ([help](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/15045592337180-How-do-I-get-paid-with-Quick-Stores), [min](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/15045657029020-Is-there-a-minimum-Quick-Stores-order-payout)).

**Printify Pop-Up Store.** The URL is `name.printify.me`. **If you rename the store, the old URL dies with no redirect.** You get a logo, social links, a theme, and an optional custom domain. Printify handles support and payments, and doesn't accept returns or exchanges ([help JSON](https://help.printify.com/api/v2/help_center/en-us/articles/12051129478417.json)). Payouts arrive on the 15th of the following month by Stripe bank transfer. There's no minimum in the US, CA, AU and NZ, and Stripe holds the first payout for 7 days ([help JSON](https://help.printify.com/api/v2/help_center/en-us/articles/12051908094993.json)). Printify says "you won't be charged for any products." A 5% transaction fee reported by third parties is **[unconfirmed]** ([podvector](https://podvector.ai/articles/printify/costs-and-charges/printify-pop-up-store-free-full-breakdown-for-pod-sellers)). The catalog has several cut-and-sew AOP hoodies with a choice of print providers ([Printify AOP](https://printify.com/all-over-print/)).

**Redbubble.** A marketplace, not a brand. Your shop is a profile. From 2025, platform fees on monthly earnings are 50% for Standard, 20% for Premium and 0% for Pro. Standard and Premium also pay an "excess markup fee" on markup above 20% ([help](https://help.redbubble.com/hc/en-us/articles/50959494599060-How-does-the-platform-fee-get-calculated) *(snippet)*; [blog](https://blog.redbubble.com/2025/08/excess-markup-fee-explained/)). A 50% rate on that excess is **[unconfirmed]**. Payouts land by the 15th ([help](https://help.redbubble.com/hc/en-us/articles/202270799) *(snippet)*).

**Threadless Artist Shops.** Free. The artist sets the price and Threadless deducts a base fee. The retail price includes a 5% processing fee. Payouts are monthly via PayPal. "Managed Pricing" guarantees at least 10% during promotions ([policy](https://artistshopshelp.threadless.com/article/207-what-is-your-policy-on-payment-of-artist-earnings)). You can customize fonts, colours, the banner and the logo ([help](https://artistshopshelp.threadless.com/article/20-how-do-i-customize-my-artist-shop-s-design)). Custom domain support is inconsistent in Threadless's own help ([help](https://artistshopshelp.threadless.com/article/902-can-i-use-a-custom-domain-name)) **[unconfirmed]**. Marketplace sales have earned a flat 10% royalty since 2025-02-03 ([Threadless](https://creativeresources.threadless.com/no-creator-fees-at-threadless-artist-shops-heres-why/) *(snippet)*).

**Shopify creator and AI flows.** Summer '25 Edition (May 2025) added Horizon block themes, an AI store builder that turns a description into layout options, and AI block generation ([Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/05/21/shopify-summer-25-edition-introduces-horizon-a-new-standard-for-creative-control/), secondary). Collabs (creator affiliate payouts) is included on paid plans, with a 2.9% fee on commission payouts ([Omnisend](https://www.omnisend.com/blog/shopify-collabs/)) **[unconfirmed by Shopify]**.

**AI-native tools.**
- **Brandify** launched on Product Hunt on 2025-11-25. It offers "build your brand, create designs, plan drops, make content, generate mockups" and an instant storefront. Pricing and fulfilment are unknown ([PH](https://www.producthunt.com/products/brandify-4/launches)).
- **Vaybel** is a Shopify app for AI designs, mockups and listings, integrating Printful and Printify. It has a $19/mo Starter plan ([app](https://apps.shopify.com/vaybel)) *(snippet)*.
- **Raspberry AI** is B2B fashion-design genAI. It raised a $24M Series A led by a16z in January 2025, and its clients include Under Armour ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/13/raspberry-ai-raises-24m-from-a16z-to-accelerate-fashion-design)). It has no storefront.
- **Off/Script** (launched 2023) puts community-voted designs into pre-orders that it then manufactures. Its creator split is reported variously as 20% plus $500, or 70% **[conflicting]** ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/11/21/off-script-launches-ai-app)).
- **Resleeve** makes AI clothing-line design tools, with marketing dated 2024 ([site](https://resleeve.ai/from-trend-follower-to-trendsetter-create-your-own-ai-generated-clothing-brand-with-resleeve/)).

## 2. What separates "launch a brand" from "sell a shirt"

1. **Brand as a first-class object, separate from the person.** Fourthwall's separate shops, each with its own payouts and catalog, is the right shape. Spring's one-store-per-account rule is the anti-pattern.
2. **Identity kit**: name, logo and wordmark, palette, type and voice, applied across the storefront, emails and **the garment itself**. Printful sells inside and outside labels for AOP, so a brand's own neck label is buyable.
3. **Collections and drops** with launch dates. Bonfire's campaigns and Fourthwall's per-collection layouts show drops are the native rhythm of merch.
4. **Stable public address.** A clean slug or custom domain that survives renames. Printify breaking URLs on rename is the cautionary tale.
5. **Transparent margin and predictable payouts.** Every platform shows base cost plus margin. The norm is monthly, about $25 minimum, Stripe Connect.
6. **Team / collaborators** on a brand (Fourthwall seats, Shopify staff).
7. **A real product range**, meaning full garments and not just a tee with a graphic. Only the Printful and Printify catalogs expose cut-and-sew tracksuits.
8. **Help getting found and making content**: mockups, lookbooks, social. Brandify and Vaybel sell exactly this.

## 3. Gaps Street Royalty could own

- **An AI brand designer that turns identity into product.** Shopify generates a theme from a description, Raspberry designs garments for enterprises, and Vaybel makes listings. Nobody takes "who you are" and turns it into a brand kit, a coordinated AOP collection, a store and a printed label in one flow.
- **A full outfit as the product unit.** The Printful + Shopify stack sells cut-and-sew tracksuits, but every creator platform treats products as single SKUs. Nobody sells a coordinated jacket-plus-pants set designed together.
- **Non-technical makers.** Shopify plus Printful is powerful but complex. Fourthwall and Spring are simple but generic. Nobody serves a first-time founder with no design skills who wants a weird, fashion-forward label.
- **Brand-level collaboration and splits.** No platform found offers per-brand revenue splits between co-founders or designers. Fourthwall only splits payouts by store and requires separate Stripe accounts.
- **Physical brand identity.** Branded neck labels or tags are possible at Printful, but no creator platform puts them in the brand kit.

## 4. Recommendations for the brand spec

**Data model.** Today's tables are `profiles`, `designs`, `collections` and `garment_sets`.

- `brands`: `id`, `slug` (unique, lowercase), `name`, `tagline`, `story`, `status` (`draft|live|paused`), `logo_asset_id`, `wordmark_asset_id`, `label_asset_id` (art for the printed neck label), `kit` (jsonb: palette tokens, fonts, voice, motifs; the AI designer reads it as constraints), `theme` (jsonb: preset id plus block config), `custom_domain` (nullable, for later), `created_by`, timestamps.
- `brand_slug_history(brand_id, slug)`: old slugs 301-redirect to the current one. This avoids the Printify failure.
- `brand_members(brand_id, user_id, role: owner|editor|viewer, revenue_share_bps)`: many brands per maker and many makers per brand from day one, even if v1 only exposes the owner.
- `collections.brand_id` plus `drop_at` and `status`: collections become drops.
- `designs.brand_id` nullable: personal designs can be "adopted" into a brand. `garment_sets.brand_id` makes a tracksuit (jacket 801 plus pants 618) one sellable look.
- `listings(brand_id, design_or_set_id, price, base_cost_snapshot, margin)`: the maker sees margin live, as on every competitor.
- `payout_accounts(owner_type: brand|user, stripe_account_id)`: Stripe Connect is the norm (Fourthwall, Printify, Printful Quick Stores). Recommend one connected account per brand owner, with brand-level splits computed in the ledger. Copy the monthly, ~$25-minimum cadence, but treat it as a product decision. Fees and terms come from Stripe and still need confirming.

**Storefront.**

- Public URL `/b/{slug}`, with a custom domain later (Fourthwall gives Pro users one free).
- Use preset themes plus block slots generated from the brand kit (Horizon-style), not free-form HTML. That keeps every store good-looking for non-technical makers.
- Required v1 sections: hero/lookbook, current drop with countdown, collections, "the maker" story, and products that show the full outfit.
- Print the brand's label on AOP pieces by default, and show it on the product page as proof it's a real label.
- Avoid: per-tier platform fees that punish new sellers (Redbubble's 50%), one store per account (Spring), URLs that break on rename (Printify), and setting up two separate SaaS tools (Shopify plus Printful).
