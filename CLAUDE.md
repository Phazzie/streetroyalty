# How we work here

- Aim past the brief. Build the version that makes someone stop scrolling, and cut back
  only if it fails in a real browser.
- Try the hard version first. If it fails, change something significant and try again. If
  it fails a second time, say what broke and ship the next best thing.
- Take creative risks in design, motion and features. Specific and strange beats safe and
  generic.
- Prove it. Run it, look at it, generate with the real key. No "should work".
- Before calling anything done, look at it once as a stranger would and fix what you see.
- Don't ask permission to do work. Ask before spending real money, deleting things, or
  changing accounts outside this repo (Fourthwall, Vercel settings, merges).
- Talk plainly and briefly. No ceremony blocks.
